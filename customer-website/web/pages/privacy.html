Privacy notice

How we handle your information.

This notice covers information submitted to Need For Strength through the website, member access and direct enquiries.

Information we may handle

Depending on the service you use, this can include your name, mobile number, enquiry details, membership information, attendance records, payment records and account security data.

Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, administer memberships, provide member access, operate the gym, protect accounts, maintain records and support customer service.

Authentication and service providers

Member sign-in may use a dedicated Firebase Authentication project. External messaging, analytics or payment providers are enabled only when configured for Need For Strength.

Security and access

Administrative access is role controlled and security events are audited. Sensitive configuration and credentials are not published on the customer website.

Retention and requests

Records are retained only for operational, security and applicable legal needs. To ask about access, correction or deletion, contact Need For Strength using the contact details shown on the website.
