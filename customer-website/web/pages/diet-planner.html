Indian plate guardrails

Keep vegetables, fruit and variety non-negotiable.

  • Aim for vegetables and fruit across the day.
  • Prefer whole grains, millets, pulses and minimally processed foods.
  • Use iodised salt sparingly and keep fried/packaged foods occasional.
Progress rule

Adjust from trends, not one weigh-in.

  • Track a 7-day average for 2–3 weeks.
  • Review hunger, energy and gym performance.
  • Change calories gradually with qualified guidance.
